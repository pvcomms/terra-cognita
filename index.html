Thesis01

A subject-object machine

Kegan's self-authorship is the move from things you are to things you have. A belief you are made of is invisible; you look through it. A belief rendered as a landform is something you stand beside and appraise. A map does this to a landscape you are standing in. The instrument converts subject into object one structure at a time, then lets the person decide what to do with the object. That deciding act, Frankfurt's second-order volition, is implemented as a button with a timestamp.

The claim is not that a person has a true self waiting to be found. The claim is that a person can be made legible to themselves, that legibility is measurable, and that a legible self makes better-calibrated judgements.

The map is hardware, not metaphor. The hippocampal-entorhinal system that maps physical space also maps conceptual space and social space specifically, along axes of power and affiliation. The method of loci works because spatial memory is the strongest memory system we have. Putting "relationship with father" at a location recruits machinery built for locations.

Two perspectives, both documented. Memories come in a field perspective (through your own eyes) or an observer perspective (watching yourself). Observer lowers intensity and shifts meaning toward the abstract; self-distancing turns a harmful "why" into a useful one. But observer perspective on trauma memories is also associated with avoidance. So there are two modes with a clinical rationale, and they alternate: Observer for meaning, Field for concrete processing.

Coherence has four kinds. Habermas and Bluck: temporal, causal-motivational, thematic, and the cultural concept of biography. They map one-to-one onto the four surfaces above: the timeline, the edges, the districts, and the outer-circumstance lanes. The primary outcome measure already exists: Campbell's self-concept clarity, "the extent to which self-beliefs are clearly and confidently defined, internally consistent, and temporally stable."

Ideology02

Five reframes, stated plainly

"Strip away" is the wrong verb. Social mediation is not a film on top of a pure self; it is constitutive. The self is dialogical (Taylor, Mead, Vygotsky). Taste is habitus, "embodied history, internalised as second nature and so forgotten as history" (Bourdieu). Preferences are constructed at the moment of asking (Lichtenstein and Slovic; Ariely's coherent arbitrariness). There is nothing underneath to strip down to. The achievable goal is better: make the mediation visible, tag its source, and let the person re-author the weights. A preference that came from a first manager and would not survive anonymity is not deleted. It is relabelled, and the person decides. Stiegler's pharmakon is the frame: the same technics that proletarianise memory can capacitate it, depending on who owns the record. Local-first is the pharmacological choice, not a feature.

"True self" is a folk concept with a known bias. People across cultures believe the true self is morally good, regardless of evidence (Strohminger, Knobe and Newman). An instrument promising to find it will be pulled toward flattery by the concept itself. Treat it as a regulative ideal. The operational replacements are measurable: Higgins' actual/ideal/ought gaps, Ryan and Connell's locus of regulation from external through introjected and identified to integrated, Sheldon and Elliot's self-concordance. "Alignment" here means shrinking the actual-ought gap on endorsed values and moving them toward integrated regulation.

Narrative is a tool, not a virtue. Ricoeur and McAdams put narrative identity at the centre and Adler shows agency in narrative predicting mental-health gains. Strawson's "Against Narrativity" is the honest counterweight: many people are episodic, not diachronic, and forcing a story on them harms. Over-coherence smooths contradiction and forecloses. So: atlas, not autobiography. Gaps and contradictions are first-class objects. Coherence is offered, scored, and refusable.

Humans are the instrument, not the bottleneck. The bottleneck is feedback. Life gives almost none on judgement, which makes taste and self-knowledge "wicked" learning environments (Hogarth; Kahneman and Klein). The instrument's real job is manufacturing kind ones: blind re-tests, delayed disclosure, forecast scoring. Compute is not the cost. Attention is, and rumination is the risk.

Built against the Hook model. No streaks, no variable reward, no notification loop. Success metric: the person needs it less. The atlas is portable, exportable, deletable. Glissant's right to opacity: regions may be left unmapped on purpose, and the map records the refusal as a refusal. Foucault's hupomnemata, the Ignatian examen, Franklin's ledger: the instrument is old. What is new is instrumentation, scale, and the ability to compute over the record.

Epistemic states. Nothing enters the map as a fact. Reported: the person said it, in these words, at this age of telling. Proposed: the extractor inferred it, with receipts; drawn dashed, contributes half mass until endorsed. Endorsed: the person performed an explicit act. Contested: the person disputes it; it stays on the map, red-dotted, because the dispute is data. Retracted: removed from rendering, kept in the log. Every transition is an event with who, when and why.

Temporal model. Valid time (when it formed) and transaction time (when it was told). Reinforcements are dated and weighted. Recuts are dated. Unconformities are intervals with a kind. Mass at time t is base plus the sum of reinforcement intensities up to t; elevation is a density over mass, which is why scrubbing the age above makes the continent grow.

Multi-observer. An account is one observer's atlas. A shared event is anchored to an index fossil. Displacement is the vector between where the event sits in each account, drawn as a fault. Shared bedrock is a belief present in both accounts from different sides. Rules: separate elicitation first, consent per node, no merge, no adjudication.

Mechanics05

How the instrument moves

  • Capture. Local voice. Whisper-class transcription on device, diarised, with paralinguistic tags (pause length, laughter, pitch, trail-offs) because the register in which something is said is part of what was said. A local extractor proposes nodes and edges, each with at least one receipt, into a queue the person endorses, contests or edits. Interview scaffolds per type: McAdams' Life Story Interview for events; downward-arrow laddering for beliefs to schemas; PVQ or Valued Living Questionnaire for values vocabulary, then locus items; functional analysis for strategies; the triadic protocol for tastes; public timelines plus household events for circumstances.
  • Layout. The person hand-places a few landmarks first; placing is elicitation. Embeddings over receipts lay out the rest, constrained by anchors, then a force relaxation with district gravity. The projection is a control and its distortion is declared.
  • Cartography. Elevation from weighted mass density; hypsometric tints; coastline at the mass-one threshold, so an unreinforced structure is an islet. Districts are Voronoi cells merged by domain. Generalisation by zoom. Silences hatched. Terra incognita where the model suspects structure but has no receipts, labelled with the question it would ask. North is the declared orientation value.
  • Timeline. Clock and proportional scales. Braudel lanes. Unconformities across all lanes. Reminiscence bump shaded, because density there is expected. Recorded-at ticks. The scrubber is a cross-section plane.
  • Core sample. Per node: strata as bands (thickness is intensity, tint is the event's district, oldest at bottom), intrusions as diagonal cuts, erosion listed, receipts, edges in and out with type and rung, status, locus, mass, years unnamed.
  • Observer / Field. Observer: whole continent, third-person prompts. Field: the map re-renders as of the node's age, fog to the neighbourhood, camera moves in, receipts in first-person present, sensory prompts, time-boxed, an exit ritual back. Trauma-flagged nodes require consent or a clinician flag before Field.
  • Role-play. Speak as a schema (IFS unblending) or to a person node (empty chair). The map is the stage. The transcript becomes receipts tagged dialogue, dated today: evidence of the present relation, not the past.
  • Compare. Separate elicitation, then both atlases side by side, shared events by index fossil, faults drawn, facies columns side by side, shared bedrock highlighted, the outer lanes shared. The conversation, if it happens, happens with both maps open.
  • Calibration loop. The self-model forecasts choices and feelings; the person acts; Brier accumulates. The person also forecasts themselves, scored separately. Where forecasts fail, the map is wrong somewhere, and the failure points at the district.
  • Contradiction surfacing. Contradicts edges listed per district with receipts. Three moves offered: endorse one side, hold both as an unexamined unconformity with a note, or recut with a new telling. Negative capability is an allowed answer.
The wedge06

Taste and discernment first

Cheapest to test on yourself in a week. Strongest experimental literature of any module. No trauma gating. It produces the first landmass and every measure the atlas will later need, and it is a product people already want and cannot get from any recommender, because no recommender's objective is theirs.

  1. Element pool. Three or four domains, 20–24 elements each. Half named by the person, half imported from actual choices where available. Stated and revealed sit side by side from the first minute.
  2. Triadic elicitation. Three elements: which two are alike, and how; how does the third differ. Ten to fifteen triads per domain. Output: bipolar constructs in the person's own words. Minimal reason-giving.
  3. Laddering. Per construct, "why does that side matter to you?" until it stops changing. Superordinate constructs are values and connect to value nodes.
  4. Grid rating. Every element on every construct. Principal components give the construct space. Each element has coordinates. The Taste district's layout is computed, not drawn.
  5. Provenance. Per element and construct: first encounter (age, via whom); the internalised audience ("who do you imagine watching when you choose this"); "would you still choose it if no one ever knew"; locus items.
  6. Blind re-rating. Strip identifiers where feasible and re-rate. The delta between labelled and blind is the mediation signal per element. (Plassmann's wine; Lee, Frederick and Ariely's beer.)
  7. Disclosure timing. For new elements, randomise whether prestige information arrives before or after the first rating. Estimate the person's susceptibility and show it.
  8. Hume rubric. Practice, comparison, delicacy, prejudice, sense: self-rated per domain. Where discernment is trained and where it is borrowed. Bourdieu's caveat printed beside it.
  9. Forecasts. The grid predicts the person's choice on ten fresh triads; the person chooses; Brier. Weekly thereafter, the person forecasts themselves; both scored.
  10. Output. The construct map, a mediation report, the list of constructs used but never named, and per element an endorse / retract / hold prompt.

Ethics. No normativity of taste. No ranking of people. No "good taste" score. A preference the person keeps after seeing where it came from is a legitimate outcome; it is now chosen. Evaluation. Test-retest of constructs at two and four weeks, forecast accuracy over time, self-concept clarity pre and post, and the change in the blind-versus-labelled delta for endorsed items.

Risks08

Ten ways this fails, and the structural answer to each

  • Confabulation. A model will generate a coherent, moving, false causal story. Answer: receipts, layers, half-mass for proposals, person-only endorsement, no counterfactual rung without the person's counterfactual.
  • Suggestion and false memory. No leading prompts. Prompts are open, sensory and logged, so an interview can be audited for suggestion.
  • Iatrogenic rumination. Register detection in transcripts, mode alternation, time boxes, the Ruminative Responses Scale tracked as a safety measure.
  • Over-coherence and foreclosure. Unconformities first-class; no arc required; contradictions kept.
  • The moral true-self bias. Named on the page. Measured with discrepancy and locus, never a "true self" score.
  • Self-exploitation. Anti-engagement design, opacity rights, export and delete.
  • Compare mode as a weapon. Consent per node, no merge, separate elicitation, and the instrument refuses to render one account without the other's consent.
  • Cultural specificity. The Western individual is not the default self (Markus and Kitayama). Districts and elicitation are configurable.
  • Measurement gaming. Performed coherence does not predict. Forecast scoring is the check.
  • Data sensitivity. The most sensitive data a person owns. Local-first, encrypted at rest. The private protocol is assumed here and nothing works without it.

Measures. Primary: self-concept clarity at intake, four and twelve weeks. Secondary: RRS, Higgins discrepancies, locus distribution, narrative coherence coded per Reese et al., forecast Brier, metacognitive sensitivity, years unnamed, and for dyads the perspective-taking subscale of the IRI. Design: n-of-1 first, then a small within-subject series, then a waitlist comparison, with measures pre-registered before the second person uses it.
