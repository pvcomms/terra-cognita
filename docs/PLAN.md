# Terra Cognita — the plan, summarised

This is a two-page summary of a v0.1 design document dated 2026-09-17. It is scaffolding, not
substance: a map of the territory the instrument is trying to occupy, written before most of it
was built. The page in this repository is a prototype of the parts marked v1 below, running on
invented data.

**Every citation here is unverified.** The source document states plainly that each reference
is given from memory as author, year and venue, and that nothing in it was checked against a
primary source. Nothing has been checked since. Treat every name below as a pointer to look up,
not as a claim that was confirmed, and verify before citing any of it anywhere. Findings the
source flagged as contested in the literature are flagged here too. The source document itself
is not published: it is written as a letter to one person about his own brief and his own life,
and that part is his.

## The claim

Not that a person has a true self waiting to be found. That a person can be made **legible to
themselves**, that legibility is measurable, and that a legible self makes better-calibrated
judgements and can endorse or retract the values it finds itself holding.

Kegan's self-authorship is the move from things you *are* to things you *have*. A belief you
are made of is invisible; you look through it. A belief rendered as a landform is something you
stand beside and appraise. The instrument is a machine for converting subject into object, one
structure at a time. Frankfurt's second-order volition — wanting to want something — is
implemented as a button with a timestamp.

The map is not decoration. The hippocampal-entorhinal system that maps physical space also maps
conceptual and social space (Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens 2016; Bellmund et al. 2018;
Tavares et al. 2015). Putting a relationship at a location recruits machinery built for
locations.

## Four reframes the design insists on

**"Strip away the mediation" is the wrong verb.** Social mediation is not a film on top of a
pure self; it is constitutive. Taste is habitus — embodied history, forgotten as history
(Bourdieu). Preferences are constructed at the moment of asking (Lichtenstein & Slovic; Ariely
et al.). There is nothing underneath to strip down to. The achievable goal is to make the
mediation visible, tag its source, and let the person re-author the weights. A preference that
came from a first manager and would not survive anonymity is not deleted; it is relabelled, and
the person decides.

**"True self" is a folk concept with a known bias.** People across cultures believe the true
self is morally good regardless of the evidence (Strohminger, Knobe & Newman). An instrument
promising to find it will be pulled toward flattering findings by the concept itself. The
operational replacements are measurable: Higgins' actual/ideal/ought discrepancies, Ryan and
Connell's locus of regulation from external through introjected and identified to integrated,
Campbell's self-concept clarity as the primary outcome.

**Narrative is a tool, not a virtue.** Ricoeur and McAdams put narrative identity at the centre;
Strawson's "Against Narrativity" is the counterweight, arguing that many people are episodic
rather than diachronic and that forcing a story on them is a harm. Over-coherence smooths
contradiction and forecloses (Bruner). So: **atlas, not autobiography**. Gaps and contradictions
are first-class objects. Coherence is offered, scored and refusable.

**Built against the engagement model, not with it.** No streaks, no variable reward, no
notification loop. The success metric is that the person needs it less. Glissant's right to
opacity: regions may be left unmapped on purpose, and the map records the refusal as a refusal
rather than as a gap to fill.

## Where the vocabulary comes from

**Cartography.** Every map is a projection and every projection distorts; choose it and declare
it (Monmonier, Harley). What survives at each zoom is a decision (Töpfer & Pillewizer;
Shneiderman's overview first, zoom and filter, details on demand). Silences are arguments, so
they are drawn (Harley). Lynch's five elements are the legend: paths are strategies, edges are
boundaries, districts are domains of life, nodes are decision points, landmarks are pivotal
events.

**Geology.** The phenomenologists were already using the metaphor — Husserl's sedimentation,
Merleau-Ponty's habit body, Bourdieu's habitus, Freud's Rome. The instrument takes the actual
laws. Superposition: what lies above was laid down later, so strata are reinforcing events,
oldest at the bottom (Steno). Cross-cutting: a reinterpretation is younger than what it cuts,
so a recut is drawn as a dated intrusion that does not erase. Unconformity: a gap in the record
is an object with a kind — no-record, refused, unexamined (Hutton). Facies: the same event lays
down different rock for different observers (Walther), which is why compare mode never merges
two accounts.

**Temporality.** Duration is not clock time, so the axis has two scales and the proportional one
is logarithmic in age (Bergson; Janet; Lemlich). Ages 10 to 30 are shaded because density there
is expected rather than a finding (Rubin, Wetzler & Nebes, the reminiscence bump). Three
durations — structure, conjuncture, event — become the three outer lanes (Braudel). Valid time
and transaction time are separate, so every node carries when it formed and when it was first
said, and the gap is a metric: years unnamed (Snodgrass). The log is the truth and state is
derived; retraction is an event, nothing is deleted.

**Memory.** Memory is reconstructive (Bartlett; Loftus; Schacter), so every memory is stored as
a *telling*, dated by when it was told, and repeated tellings are separate rows. Field and
observer perspectives are both documented and do different work: observer lowers intensity and
abstracts, field processes concretely, and observer perspective on trauma is also associated
with avoidance (Nigro & Neisser; Libby & Eibach; Kenny & Bryant). Hence two modes that alternate
rather than one default. Constructive versus unconstructive repetitive thought (Watkins) is the
line between the instrument working and the instrument becoming a rumination engine.

**Judgement.** Calibration is scorable (Brier; Tetlock), so the self-model makes forecasts about
the person and reality scores them: the atlas earns authority by prediction rather than
eloquence. Intuition is trustworthy only in kind environments (Hogarth; Kahneman & Klein), and
taste is a wicked one, so the instrument manufactures kindness with blind re-tests, delayed
disclosure and forecast scoring. Constructs can be elicited without being named (Kelly's triads,
Hinkle's laddering). Causation carries a rung — association, intervention, counterfactual
(Pearl) — and a counterfactual rung requires the person's own counterfactual sentence.

## The ontology, in one paragraph

Six core node types — event, person, belief, schema, value, strategy — plus taste and
circumstance. Seven edge types: caused, reinforced, contradicts, inherited, protects, recut,
mediates. Five epistemic states: reported, proposed, endorsed, contested, retracted, where
nothing enters as a fact, proposals are drawn at half mass until a person endorses them, and a
contested item stays on the map because the dispute is data. Mass at time *t* is the base plus
the intensities of reinforcements up to *t*; elevation is a density over mass, which is why
scrubbing time makes the continent form.

## Staged, and gated

| Stage       | Scope                                                                | Gate                                                                |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| v0 Taste    | The discernment protocol; renders the taste district only            | Constructs stable at retest; forecasts beat chance                  |
| v1 Atlas    | All six core types, voice capture, timeline, observer mode, core sample | Self-concept clarity rises; no rise in rumination                 |
| v2 Inhabit  | Field mode, role-play, contradiction surfacing, calibration loop      | Field sessions time-boxed and rated; alternation shown to help      |
| v3 Compare  | Dyads: separate elicitation, shared events, faults, consent per node  | Two people sharing a hard event report better understanding, no more conflict |
| v4 Commons  | Opt-in aggregated circumstance layer, no individual data              | Only after v3 and a real privacy review                             |

The taste module is first because it is the cheapest to test, has the strongest experimental
literature, needs no trauma gating, and produces every measure the rest of the atlas will need.

## How it is expected to fail

Confabulation: a model will generate a coherent, moving, false causal story. The answer is
structural — receipts required, proposals at half mass, person-only endorsement, no
counterfactual rung without the person's own counterfactual. Suggestion and false memory: no
leading prompts, and prompts logged so an interview can be audited. Iatrogenic rumination: the
tool can become the thing it fights, so register detection, mode alternation and time boxes,
with a rumination scale tracked as a safety measure. Over-coherence: unconformities are
first-class and no arc is required. Compare mode as a weapon: separate elicitation, consent per
node, no merge, no adjudication. Measurement gaming: performed coherence does not predict, and
forecast scoring is the check. And the obvious one: this is the most sensitive data a person
owns, so local-first is not a feature.

## What the page in this repository actually is

The v1 surfaces, running on invented data: the map with its districts and typed edges, the
timeline with both scales and the three outer lanes, the core sample, observer and field modes,
the mediation hatch, and a two-account compare overlay. Capture, the extractor, the calibration
loop, the taste protocol, persistence and every gate above are unbuilt.

Open questions the design leaves open: whether voice capture carries the expressive-writing
effect or needs a written pass; what the right district set is for an interdependent
self-construal; whether a local model is good enough to propose schemas; and what a person
should see first, the map or the timeline — the document's own hypothesis being the timeline,
because it is honest and the map is seductive.
